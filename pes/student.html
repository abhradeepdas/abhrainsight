Marks & Points Breakdown

See exactly how each test score is calculated.

Breakdown will appear here.
1
Base Points
Your obtained marks are converted to a score out of 10.
Base Points = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 10
2
Highest Marks Bonus
If you qualify for the highest-marks bonus, 0.30 points are added.
Highest Marks Bonus = +0.30 points
3
Improvement Bonus
If you qualify for the improvement bonus, 0.20 points are added.
Improvement Bonus = +0.20 points
4
Points Before Cap
This is the total score after adding the Base Points and all eligible bonuses, before applying the 10-point maximum.
Points Before Cap = Base Points + Highest Marks Bonus + Improvement Bonus
5
10-Point Cap
The active score for one test cannot be more than 10 points.
Active Score = minimum(Points Before Cap, 10)
6
Points Vault
If Points Before Cap is greater than 10, the excess points are stored in the Points Vault.
Vault Deposit = max(Points Before Cap − 10, 0)
7
Using the Points Vault
If a later test has a score below 10, available Vault points can be used to raise that score, up to the 10-point limit.
Final Points = Active Score + Vault Used
8
Final Points
Final Points are the points recorded for that test after applying the cap and any eligible Points Vault protection.
Final Points ≤ 10.00
9
Student Average
Your average is calculated from the Final Points of all published tests included in your record.
Average = Total Final Points ÷ Number of Published Tests
