🏆 RECOGNITION & AWARDS

PRIZES & CERTIFICATES

Official recognition based on final average score, rank and attendance.

🏆

Champion Trophy

Awarded to the Rank 1 holder on the final leaderboard only if they secure a minimum average score of 7.50 Points or above.

If Rank 1 falls below 7.50 Points, no Champion Trophy is awarded.
🥇

Performance Trophy

Awarded to every student who maintains an average score of 8.00 Points or above.

If Rank 1 achieves 8.00+ Points, they earn both the Champion Trophy and the Performance Trophy.
🎓

Certificate of Excellence

Awarded to top rank holders maintaining an average score of 8.50 Points or above.

📜

Merit Certificate

Awarded to every student maintaining an average score between 7.50 and 8.49 Points.

⏱️

100% Attendance & Punctuality Certificate

An additional, dedicated certificate awarded to any student who attends every exam on schedule.

A student can earn two certificates in total if they qualify for both academic performance and full attendance.
